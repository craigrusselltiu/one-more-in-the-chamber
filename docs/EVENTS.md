# Events

Random events encountered at event nodes on the map. Each event presents 2-3 choices with different risk/reward tradeoffs.

## The Card Game
- Flavor: "Raccoon in a top hat. Three cards face down. 'Ten gold a card, friend.'"
- Choices:
    - **Play** -- -10 gold, pick a card. Artifact, consumable, tile type, or nothing.
    - **Leave**

## The Wanted Board
- Flavor: "Your face on a poster. Pretty good likeness."
- Choices:
    - **Tear down** -- Normalise merchant prices.
    - **Leave up** -- +30 gold, next elite +20% HP.

## The Snake Charmer
- Flavor: "Hooded figure, swaying snakes. Offers to let one bite you."
- Choices:
    - **Bite** -- -10 HP, gain Rattlesnake-tagged artifact.
    - **Decline** -- +10 gold.

## The Abandoned Mine
- Flavor: "Sign reads 'KEEP OUT.' Naturally, you go in."
- Choices:
    - **Go deeper** -- Risk HP for artifact chance.
    - **Leave** -- Keep what you have.

## The Broken Cart
- Flavor: "Merchant's cart, busted wheel, goods scattered."
- Choices:
    - **Help** -- -1 consumable, merchants stock +1 item.
    - **Rob** -- +2 consumables, +15 gold, next merchant +20%.

## The Old Well
- Flavor: "Deep well. Something jingling below."
- Choices:
    - **Climb** -- -10 HP, +30 gold + random consumable.
    - **Bucket** -- +15 gold.

## The Dynamite Stash
- Flavor: "Crate of unstable dynamite."
- Choices:
    - **Take carefully** -- +2 Stick of TNT.
    - **Blow it up** -- Skip next node, -15 HP.

## The Traveling Preacher
- Flavor: "Man in black at a crossroads. Bible in one hand, revolver in the other. 'Confess your sins, child.'"
- Choices:
    - **Confess** -- Lose your highest-tier artifact, heal to full HP.
    - **Draw** -- -15 HP. Gain random Gunslinger-tagged artifact.
    - **Walk away** -- +5 block for next fight.

## The Dust Devil
- Flavor: "Swirling column of sand barreling toward you. Something glints inside it."
- Choices:
    - **Ride it out** -- -10 HP. Board starts shuffled next fight.
    - **Reach in** -- -20 HP. Gain artifact + random consumable.
    - **Take cover** -- No effect.

## The Ghost Town Saloon
- Flavor: "Swinging doors on an empty bar. Dusty piano plays itself. Full bottle of whiskey on the counter."
- Choices:
    - **Drink** -- Heal 20 HP. Next fight: -1 swap.
    - **Search the back** -- 50% chance: random artifact. 50% chance: ambush, -15 HP.
    - **Move on** -- +5 gold from the tip jar.

## The Stranded Prospector
- Flavor: "Old-timer half-buried in a collapsed mineshaft. 'Get me outta here and I'll make it worth your while.'"
- Choices:
    - **Dig him out** -- -5 HP from exertion. +25 gold, upgrade a random tile.
    - **Take his gear** -- +2 random consumables. Merchants cost +10% this act.

## The Campfire Stranger
- Flavor: "Figure by a fire. Hat pulled low. 'Sit a spell. Trade stories?'"
- Choices:
    - **Sit down** -- Heal 15 HP. Gain intel: see enemy intents next fight.
    - **Trade** -- Give 1 consumable, receive 2 random consumables.
    - **Keep walking** -- +1 swap next fight.

## The Train Wreck
- Flavor: "Derailed locomotive, smoke still rising. Cargo scattered across the gulch."
- Choices:
    - **Loot the cargo** -- +20 gold, +1 random consumable.
    - **Search the engine** -- -10 HP from debris. Pick 1 of 3 artifacts.
    - **Check for survivors** -- Next merchant: all items -25%.

## The Vulture Circle
- Flavor: "Vultures circling something in the brush. It's a dead bounty hunter. Still has his gear."
- Choices:
    - **Take the gear** -- Gain random artifact. Next elite gains +1 ability.
    - **Bury him** -- Heal 10 HP, +10 gold from his pockets.

## The Rigged Bridge
- Flavor: "Rope bridge over a canyon. Fraying cables. Dynamite wired to the supports."
- Choices:
    - **Defuse and cross** -- +2 Stick of TNT. -10 HP if you fail (50% chance).
    - **Blow it and climb down** -- -20 HP. Skip next combat node.
    - **Find another way** -- No risk. +5 gold found on the detour.

## The Medicine Wagon
- Flavor: "Painted wagon, jars of strange liquids. The seller's grin has too many teeth."
- Choices:
    - **Buy moonshine (-15 gold)** -- Heal 30 HP.
    - **Buy mystery vial (-10 gold)** -- Random: heal 20 HP, gain 1 Moonshine, or take 10 poison damage.
    - **Threaten him** -- +1 Moonshine, +1 Bandage. Merchants cost +15% this act.

## The Coyote Den
- Flavor: "Howling from a rocky overhang. Fresh tracks leading into a narrow cave."
- Choices:
    - **Smoke them out** -- -1 consumable. +20 gold from cached loot inside.
    - **Sneak past** -- No cost. +1 Bandage.
