# Riff -- Musical Frog Bard

## Overview

**Name:** Riff
**Species:** Frog
**Archetype:** Musician / bard
**Character ID:** `riff`
**Unlock:** Available in Reputation Shop
**HP:** 110

A laid-back amphibian who uses the power of music as his primary weapon. Riff wanders the frontier with his saxophone and banjo, turning melodies into concussive blasts and healing harmonies.

## Backstory

Riff doesn't much care for the whole "gunslinging outlaw" lifestyle. He's just trying to find the perfect acoustic spot in the canyons to play his sax. Unfortunately, the Frontier is full of folks who don't appreciate good jazz. Over time, Riff learned to channel the ambient magic of the desert through his instruments, turning a brassy honk into a shockwave that can knock a bandit flat. He strolls from town to town, playing for tips, dodging bounties, and blowing away trouble with a smooth tune.

*"Music soothes the savage beast. Or, you know, gives it a concussion. Depends on the tempo."*
