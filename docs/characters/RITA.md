# Rita -- Viper Assassin Nomad

## Overview

**Name:** Rita
**Species:** Snake (Rattlesnake/Viper)
**Archetype:** Nomad / assassin / ninja
**Character ID:** `rita`
**Unlock:** Available in Reputation Shop
**HP:** 90

A swift, lethal snake nomad who fights with dual daggers and throwing knives. Rita excels at precision strikes, poison, and overwhelming speed.

## Backstory

Rita slithers through the canyons and badlands of the Frontier, a ghost story told around campfires. She is a master of the silent kill, a former member of a secluded assassin clan who now wanders alone. Her strikes are as fast as a viper's bite, and she carries an arsenal of throwing knives that she wields with impossible accuracy. She's not a bandit or a common criminal; she's a professional. If Rita is after you, you won't hear her coming until it's already over.

*"A loud gun just tells everyone where you are. A silent blade tells them nothing."*
