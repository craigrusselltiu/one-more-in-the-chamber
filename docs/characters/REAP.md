# Reap -- Shadow Coyote Outlaw

## Overview

**Name:** Reap
**Species:** Coyote
**Archetype:** Shadow outlaw / dark magic
**Character ID:** `reap`
**Unlock:** Available in Reputation Shop
**HP:** 100

A mysterious coyote outlaw who manipulates shadows. Reap trades raw survivability for potent, dark magic and evasive maneuvers, slipping through danger like smoke.

## Backstory

Not much is known about where Reap came from before he rode into the Dusty Trail. He doesn't talk much, and when he does, it's usually with a growl and a shadow that seems to move independently of the sun. Some say he made a deal with something ancient in the deep desert; others say he was born under a cursed moon. Whatever the truth, he wields shadows like a physical weapon, wrapping them around himself to deflect bullets or lashing out to consume his enemies.

*"The dark doesn't hide you. It just gives you teeth."*

## Pixel Art Personality

- **Idle:** Tall, brooding stance, duster billowing slightly with dark shadowy wisps.
- **Attack:** Reaches out with a clawed hand, sending a wave of dark energy.
- **Hit:** Dissipates briefly into shadows before reforming.
- **Ability:** Summons a thick storm of shadows around himself.
