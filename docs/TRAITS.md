# Traits

Trait level = number of artifacts held with that tag. Effects activate at breakpoints.

## Sheriff
- Flavor: "The law is heavier than it looks."
- 2 -- Double the first time you gain block each turn.
- 4 -- At the start of combat, gain 5 Sturdy.
- 6 -- Block reflects 100% of absorbed damage back to attacker.

## Outlaw
- Flavor: "Wanted: Alive. Preferably not."
- 2 -- Killing an enemy grants 1 Rageful.
- 5 -- At the start of Boss encounters, gain 3 Rageful and apply 2 Vulnerable to all enemies.

## Prospector
- Flavor: "There's gold in them hills. And in that match."
- 2 -- On any match, 20% chance to generate 1 gold.
- 4 -- Whenever you gain gold during combat, deal 1 damage to a random enemy.
- 6 -- Deal 10% of your current gold as extra damage.

## Gunslinger
- Flavor: "Fastest hand in the west. And the luckiest."
- 2 -- Bullet, .50 Cal, Buckshot, and Ricochet tiles deal 1 extra damage per tile.
- 4 -- Gain 1 Lucky for every Bullet, .50 Cal, Buckshot, or Ricochet matched.

## Saloon Keeper
- Flavor: "On the house."
- 2 -- Consumables heal 5 HP on use (any consumable).
- 5 -- At the start of combat, gain a random consumable.

## Sapper
- Flavor: "Fire in the hole."
- 1 -- Enemy bomb timers are increased by 2.
- 5 -- Explosive tile radius is increased by 1.

## Sniper
- Flavor: "Sniper? I barely know her!"
- 4 -- On 5-match, gain 1 swap for that turn.

## Dead Man Walking
- Flavor: "I didn't hear no bell."
- 3 -- Whenever you would take damage, take 1 less damage.
- 5 -- If HP is below 20%, all damage is doubled.
- 7 -- Once per fight, taking lethal damage instead sets HP to 10 and grant 20 block.

## Rattlesnake
- Flavor: "Don't step on anything that hisses."

## Mustang
- Flavor: "Can't fence what won't be fenced."
- 4 -- +1 swap per turn. Non-adjacent swaps allowed. 5+ lasso matches: +50% damage.

## Preacher
- Flavor: "The sermon ain't over yet."
- 2 -- Healing from any source +50%.
- 4 -- Once per fight: survive lethal damage with 1 HP.
- 6 -- Enemies that die while you're above 75% HP drop 5 bonus gold.

## Tracker
- Flavor: "Read the tracks. See what's hidden."
- 1 -- Buried (sand) tiles are always visible.
- 3 -- First match each turn reveals all hazards on the board.
- 5 -- Matches adjacent to hazards deal +25% damage.

## Undertaker
- Flavor: "Business is booming."
- 2 -- Enemy death: heal 3 HP.
- 4 -- Enemy death: +2 block and +5% crit for the rest of the fight. Stacks.
- 6 -- Enemy death: next match deals 2x damage.

## Tinker
- Flavor: "Bigger boom. Better engineering."
- 2 -- Explosive tiles have +1 blast radius (5x5 cross instead of 3x3).
- 4 -- Showdown tiles clear 2 random types instead of 1.

## Drifter
- Flavor: "The wind's at your back."
- 2 -- Cascade combo multiplier +0.05x per step (instead of +0.1x per 2 steps).
- 4 -- 5+ cascade combo: gain +1 swap this turn.
- 6 -- 8+ cascade combo: all remaining matches this cascade deal 2x damage.
