# Consumables

## Offensive

### Stick of TNT
- Flavor: "Light the fuse."
- Effect: Clear the bottom row of the board. Each destroyed tile generates its single-resolve resources. Explosive and showdown chains trigger normally.
- Cost: 35

### Wanted Flyer
- Flavor: "Price on their head."
- Effect: Apply 2 Vulnerable to the targeted enemy.
- Cost: 25

### Pocket Watch
- Flavor: "Bought a little more time."
- Effect: +1 swap this turn.
- Cost: 65

### Strong Coffee
- Flavor: "Eyes sharp, hands steady."
- Effect: Double next match resources.
- Cost: 30

## Defensive

### Strong Whiskey
- Flavor: "Burns going down, patches you up."
- Effect: Heal 20 HP.
- Can use in map
- Cost: 30

### Bandage
- Flavor: "Patch wounds, suck out poison."
- Effect: Heal 10 HP and cleanse all poison tiles on the board.
- Can use in map
- Cost: 25

## Utility

### Skeleton Key
- Flavor: "Every lock has a key."
- Effect: Unlock all locked tiles on the board.
- Cost: 20

### Tumbleweed
- Flavor: "Wind rearranges everything."
- Effect: Reshuffle the entire board. Any resulting matches resolve as cascades.
- Cost: 30

### Signal Flare
- Flavor: "Nothing stays hidden."
- Effect: Reveal all Buried tiles on the board.
- Cost: 20

### Snake Oil
- Flavor: "Who knows what's in the bottle."
- Effect: Random effect (20% each): Heal 23 HP, gain 6 max HP, gain 100 gold, take 8 damage, or do nothing.
- Can use in map
- Cost: 45

### Lasso
- Flavor: "Reach across the board."
- Effect: Your next swap can target non-adjacent tiles.
- Cost: 55

### Broom
- Flavor: "Less boom, more broom.."
- Effect: Clear all tile hazards.
- Cost: 65

### Panacea
- Flavor: "Cures what ails the land itself."
- Effect: Cure all debuffs.
- Cost: 60
