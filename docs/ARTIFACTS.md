# Artifacts

## Exclusive

### Bamboo Canteen
- Rust exclusive starting artifact
- Tags: Saloon Keeper
- Rarity: Rare
- Effect: After completing combat, restore 6 HP.
- Flavor: "Bamboo doesn't rust."

### Rust's Cylinder
- Rust exclusive artifact
- Tags: Gunslinger, Outlaw
- Rarity: Legendary
- Effect: Increase Deadeye shots to 6. The last shot can now be used on an enemy, dealing 1 damage per stack of Bounty.
- Flavor: "Six in the chamber. No reloads."

### Rigged Deck
- Reno exclusive starting artifact
- Tags: Prospector
- Rarity: Rare
- Effect: Chip hits have a 50% chance to hit another enemy. Chip misses generate 2 gold.
- Flavor: "Bad luck is still luck."

### Reno's Coin
- Reno exclusive artifact
- Tags: Desperado
- Rarity: Legendary
- Effect: Chip damage is doubled and hit chance is increased to 75%. On miss, lose 1 HP.
- Flavor: "Double or nothing. Emphasis on nothing."
